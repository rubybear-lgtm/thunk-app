Thunk — Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Thunk ("the app") is a workout log for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Thunk does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following only on your device, in its private app storage:

  • Workouts you log (exercises, weights, reps, sets, notes, RPE, dates and times)
  • Bodyweight entries you choose to record
  • Plans you activate, build or change, and your settings (units, plates, strength baselines, timer preferences)

This data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. The app has no internet permission, no account system, no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.

Backups

If you use Settings → Back up, the app writes a single backup file to a location you pick with Android's file picker (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive app you have installed). Where that file goes after that is up to you and the service you choose. Restore only reads a file you select.

Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: used for the rest timer between sets and the rest-done alert.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, vibration: used so the rest timer keeps time and alerts you with the screen off.

None of these permissions are used to collect information.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear workout history or Reset logs & settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all app data from the device. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected.

Children

Thunk is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
